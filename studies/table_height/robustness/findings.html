What the ablation establishes—and leaves open

  • Reference: synchronized model, existing brace retargeting, depth 0.40 m, five-second reach gate. Precise loaded reach was 3/3 low, 3/3 nominal and 1/3 high in seeds 10–12; full-task success was 2/3, 3/3 and 0/3. The later reference block is shown separately above.
  • Model synchronization is a benchmark correctness requirement. The deliberately mismatched high-table diagnostic failed 0/3, versus 1/3 with synchronization. The saved models independently establish the geometry/keyframe mismatch. This small performance comparison does not prove that synchronization alone solves generalization.
  • Existing retargeting has a conditional, mixed result. Turning it off changed low precise successes from 3/3 to 2/3 and high from 1/3 to 2/3. It is not shown uniformly necessary or uniformly helpful.
  • A closer target is not established as necessary. Keeping the farther 0.55 m depth gave 3/3 low, 2/3 nominal and 2/3 high. Shortening the target changes task difficulty; its effect is not uniformly positive in these samples.
  • More settling time matters for the frozen endpoint. Reducing the reach gate from five seconds to two gave 0/3 precise holds at both extremes. This changes the opportunity to settle and hold; it does not improve the optimizer. The 1.98 s sampling-boundary caveat below remains explicit.

Scope of the result and stopping point

These trials test three discrete heights, a right-jaw position target at near edge +0.40 m (or the explicitly labeled +0.55 m ablation), lateral −0.04 m, and face +0.15 m, starting from home. They establish repeated loaded reaches in some conditions, with substantial remaining variation at the extremes. They do not map a reachable hemisphere, establish a continuous height window, or show that this target requires bracing. The previous CMPC 25-second holds are not directly comparable to this two-second observed MJPC endpoint.

Successful holds can approach modeled actuator limits; the physical ledger and hold-specific diagnostics retain that information. Contact penetration and foot displacement are reported rather than treated as proof of hardware feasibility. Planning runs at 167 simulated Hz and slower than real time.
